About Zigmosis AI

Zigmosis AI is an intelligent digital pet identity and healthcare platform designed to improve the safety and wellbeing of pets using artificial intelligence and modern technology.

The platform creates a unique digital identity for every pet, containing important information such as pet details, owner contact information, and health records.

Through QR-based identification and AI-powered assistance, Zigmosis helps owners manage pet information, receive healthcare guidance, and reconnect with lost pets.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a safer future for pets by connecting pet owners with smart digital solutions, AI technology, and accessible pet healthcare support.
